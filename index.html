About CNC Predictive Maintenance System

Overview

This system uses Machine Learning (Random Forest & Decision Ensemble) trained on 10,000 real-world CNC milling machine operational records (AI4I 2020 Dataset) to predict machine health, failure probability, and diagnose 5 specific mechanical failure modes.

Tool Wear Failure (TWF)

Occurs when tool wear reaches critical threshold (200-240 min), causing surface roughness and catastrophic tool shank breakage.

Heat Dissipation Failure (HDF)

Triggered when temperature difference (ΔT = Process Temp - Air Temp) is less than 8.6 K and rotational speed is below 1380 rpm.

Power Failure (PWF)

Calculated from mechanical power (P = Torque × Speed × 2π/60). Failure occurs when power is under 3500 W (spindle stall) or over 9000 W (motor overload).

Overstrain Failure (OSF)

Caused by product of Tool Wear (min) × Torque (Nm) exceeding the load limit of the machine frame (11,000 to 13,000 min·Nm depending on quality type).
